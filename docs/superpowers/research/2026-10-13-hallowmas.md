# Hallowmas Masques — research note (2026-10-13)

Prepared for `hallowmas-masques` and the `hallowmas` panel in `FallenLondon/choice-helper.js`, before Hallowmas 2026 opened. Everything is 2025's: the guide says the Masques, masks and companions change each year.

## Sources

All fetched through `fallenlondon.wiki/w/api.php` (Anubis blocks plain fetches):

- `Hallowmas (Guide)`: masks, menaces, Confessions, Masques, companion upgrades, rewards, affiliations, timeline.
- The four Masque storylet pages (The Roof Below, The Wreck of the St Elmo, The Pavilion of Butterflies, Saint Dunstan's Sacristy) and all 35 option pages they list.
- `The Fool's Judgement` (embeds Nightmares, Suspicion, Wounds, Scandal), `Hallowmas: The Fool's Justice`, and the four mask cards (`The Squall of Infants`, `The Crown's Splendour`, `The Flight of Moths`, `The Devil's Gambit`).

## Findings

- **Option pages win over the guide.** The guide's Confession-exchange table files the Wreck's three exchanges under the Pavilion and the Sacristy's three under the Wreck. The Pavilion page lists only the same two exchanges as the Roof Below. The test pins the option pages' version.
- **Per-Masque shape.** Each Masque has two options at Airs 1–33 (one needs the mask) and two at 67–100 (three in the Roof Below), the exchanges at 34–66, and two always-on options (a +1 line and a Leave). The Roof Below has a sixth Menace option (amber springs, needs Shapeling Arts 3, mutually exclusive with the vats).
- **Menace lock.** Every Menace option is locked at Menace 7; The Fool's Judgement takes it at 7 for 28 Spirit; reaching 8 sends the player to the Menace area.
- **Item chain.** Promenade! gives Absinthe, which Toast the Queen spends ×5. Walk the chambers gives Unearthly Fossil, which the vats spend. The Crown's Splendour gift gives a Volume of Collated Research, which Dine spends. Dine gives a Mourning Candle, which Donate a candle spends.
- **The Pavilion's Take a turn** is listed as Airs 0–33 on its page; transcribed as 1–33.
- **Scandal.** Not submittable in 2025; the guide says the Sacristy and the Demon return in 2026.
- **Spirit rewards.** Stated Echoes and Echoes per Spirit agree to the guide's rounding for all nine rows (462.5 / 2000 = 0.231).
- **Upgrades.** The guide's list table and tick-grid agree for all 12 upgrades.
