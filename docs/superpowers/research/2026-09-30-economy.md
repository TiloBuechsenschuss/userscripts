# Economy panel (Roof Economy, Stuiver Grinding, steel, scrip, statues) — research

Fetched through the wiki API 2026-09-30: *Roof Economy (Guide)* (10 kB), *Stuiver Grinding (Guide)* (27 kB),
*Bessemer Steel Ingot (Guide)* (30 kB), *Hinterland Scrip-Making* (52 kB), *Railway (Guide)* and *Statues at the GHR
Stations (Guide)* (skimmed), and item pages for the equipment. Nothing here is in `choice-helper.js` yet.

## 1. What it is

Reference material with **no storylet options to badge**: prices, shops, items you can only buy with Stuivers, and what
each Stuiver grind pays. The user decided (2026-09-29): **one panel, "Economy", behind ⚙ UX**, holding both Firmament guides
plus Railway steel per station, Scrip conversions and the statue table. The panel has no badge and no gate, so none of
the greeting or heading questions of a feature arise. It is filtered, searchable and built fresh on every open (`render(ctx)`).

## 4. Traps

1. **Cells are not all Stuivers.** A market cell is a Stuiver price, an Echo price, or **another item** (BSI at the Sous costs 1 Tempestuous Tale;
   Anticandle costs 250 Bone Fragments; Ratwork Mechanism sells for 1250 Moon-Pearl at Burgundy). A number field would collapse these three claims
   into one (skill: never collapse two claims). Carry `kind: 's' | 'e' | 'item'`, and `-` means "not traded here", which is not zero.
2. **Sell and buy are different columns.** `{{CenterDivide|buy|/|sell}}`: `-` on the buy side means the shop does not sell it.
3. **Guide estimates.** StPA and EPA are the guides' own figures, "assuming 20 actions per run" for the Stacks; the panel labels them the guides'
   and quotes no EPA of its own. The Stacks feature's badge quotes no EPA either; the panel figures come from the *Stuiver Grinding* text, not from it.
4. **Two of the numbers move.** Stuiver Grinding is flagged `stale=60`; the Knight count is a live template value (not carried).
5. **Statues.** The statue section is derived: if `ST_OPTIONS` changes, the panel follows. It shows only the rows that have a `rate`.
6. **No name may sit in two tables.** The panel has no gate, so the rule concerns other features only where a shared constant is reused; the panel
   must not duplicate the Stacks books or the Marrow Tale values, only link to them.
7. **Size.** Roughly 250 table rows; the panel must filter through `dataset.econSearch` (as Zailing does with `dataset.zeeSearch`) and hide group headings whose rows all went.

## 5. Decisions for the user

1. **Sections (recommended): all eight above.** Cheaper: drop 6 and 7 (steel sources and scrip), keep 1–5 and 8.
2. **What "value" means (recommended): every figure in the unit the source gives it (Stuivers, Echoes, an item), never converted**; the one
   conversion the guides state (a Tempestuous Tale is 10 Stuivers; a Scrip about 0.5 E) appears as a note. Alternative: convert everything to Stuivers.
3. **The Stuiver-only items (recommended): a table with slot, stats and the guide's note, sorted by price**, vs grouped by shop.
4. **Icon and label:** `💰` "Economy", hint "What the Roof shops pay, what only Stuivers buy, and what each grind is worth". Change if you prefer another.
5. **Statues (recommended): derived from `ST_OPTIONS`**, vs left out of this panel (they already have badges).

**Only the game can tell:** whether ⚙ UX's menu docks a fifth-or-later panel entry without crowding on the phone layout (the launcher test pins five
panel ids in `ux-launcher-docking` ("registered its five panels"); a sixth extends that list).

## 6. Decisions (2026-09-30)

1. Sections: **all eight**.
2. Unit: **everything in Stuivers.** Consequence, kept honest: a figure the source gives in another unit is converted only through a ratio the guides themselves state — 1 Echo = 20 Stuivers (Tantalising Possibility: s2 or E 0.10), 1 Tempestuous Tale = 10 s, 1 Scrip ≈ 0.5 E = 10 s — and is shown as `≈ s…` with the source figure beside it, never alone. A price paid in another **item** with no Echo or Stuiver equivalent printed by the source stays as the item.
3. Stuiver-only items: **one table by price** (shop as a column).
4. Icon and label as proposed: `💰` "Economy".
5. Statues: derived from `ST_OPTIONS`.
