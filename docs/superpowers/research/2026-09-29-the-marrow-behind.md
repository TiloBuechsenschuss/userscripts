# The Marrow Behind (Guide) — research

Fetched through the wiki API 2026-09-29: the guide (33 kB), 103 card, option and storylet pages named in
its tables, and *Return to your mooring* (the conversion). Option pages win over the guide; differences
are in §4. Nothing here is in `choice-helper.js` yet, except one story step (§5).

## 1. What it is

An Evergreen Late-Firmament activity in **Procession**, unlocked through Firmament Part 9 (In Procession 20)
and entered through *The Marrow Behind*. Three layers:

1. **Choose a form** (*Entering the Skeleton of the Sky* / *The Skeleton of the Sky*): the player is moved
   between ten "corpses" by ten form options — *Unfold yourself*, *Collapse your form*, *Become minuscule*, *Conduct
   charge*, *Insulate yourself*, *Reflect current*, *Become permeable*, *Make yourself impervious*, *Become
   insubstantial*, *Embody everything the bones desire*, and the end-state *Disappear entirely*. Each strains one of
   four aspects (enormity, conductivity, permeability, everything), and **the aspect just strained is locked
   for the next form**. *Capacity for Painful Change* starts at 3: every trip is exactly three moves and then *Re-emerge*
   (the first-ever visit ends in *Collapse* instead).
2. **A hand of three cards** in each corpse, drawn from three cards specific to it (infinite draws).
3. **Cash out** on *Distant Moorings* → *Return to your mooring* (0 actions), which turns the Marrow currencies into
   **Tempestuous Tales** by a formula printed on the page:

   `TT = 5 × Caligin Scale + 1 × Dendritic Spark + 1 × Fragmentary Transgression + 1 × Ossified Hunger + 0.02 × Unlived
   Second + 5 × Mote of Intent + 0.2 × Scrap of Tattered Void` (rounded)

   **This is the number a badge can carry.** It is a page fact, exact, and it puts every card on one axis. The guide
   values a Tale at about 0.5 E (Caligin Scale 5 TT = 2.5 E), which is the guide's own figure, not the page's.

## 3. What is not a Tale value (left to labels or to the Firmament feature)

- **The ten form options** are pure moves (they set the four form qualities and the aspect under strain). They can carry a
  **label** (`→ the Myrmidon's Corpse`) and, more usefully, say which aspect they strain; the guide's redirect table
  gives the corpse. The three shown **cards per corpse** are the value: the average Tale value of a corpse's three cards
  (§2 grouped by "Upon choosing …" in the guide) is a defensible summary, but it is a derived number nobody sees in the game.
- **Story options** that unlock a location (scars, *An Unwatchable Sight*, *An Unthinkable Thought*, *An Untenable Want*, *An
  Unknowable Light*, the three qualities the shop items need) pay a quality, not a Tale: *Defy*, *Obey your reflection*, *Remake
  yourself*, *Negotiate*, *Cut yourself out of category*, *Listen to the stillness of the wind*, *Follow her*, *Consider your own
  diminishment*, *Consume what is not there*, *Never let her go*, *Catch the boundary in your teeth*, *Forget*, *Remember the truth*,
  *Illuminate yourself with lightning*, *Meet your employer*, *Escape*. **`Consume what is not there` on *It Eats Worlds* is
  already a row of the Firmament guide feature** (`choice-helper.js:13877`, the Part 9 table); no name goes in two tables, so a
  Marrow table leaves it out. The others are not in any table. Their checks are stat checks with unread difficulty (mostly
  *Chthonosophy 50* as a narrow challenge, "you cannot actually fail" on some) and each one leaves a **failure** of a Dendritic
  Spark and Wounds +2 CP; a label with the requirement in the tooltip is honest, a number is not.
- **The exit and the first-visit ending** (*Re-emerge*, *Collapse*) and *Return to your mooring* itself.
- **The shop** (*Fulgurating Bone*: three items at about 230, 242.5 and 275 E, and two airship upgrades at 675 E) is a
  spending decision behind a Fate-free shop; the guide lists the cost, the Echo value is the guide's estimate. Not badged.
- **The three "White as …" cards** (Inkling of Identity ×1–3 with Nightmares +1–3 CP) are the Disappear entirely deck;
  not in the guide's price list, not badged.

## 5. Traps

1. **Card names the game shows are not the page titles for two cards.** The pages are `The Hunger (The Empty Corpse)` and `The
   Oath (The Empty Corpse)`; the option pages' `From Card title` reads `The Hunger` and `The Oath`. The lookup must alias the
   heading (`carouselOpen` aliases). *Escape (The Cage in Crimson)* and *Follow her (The Oath)* are the same disambiguation for options.
2. **Every card has two options, and one is often a locked story option** (needs *Discovered: The Intuition of the Estuaries*
   plus a quality). The unlocked one is the Tale option; the locked one is the story option. Badge both where they have a
   title; the Tale one carries the number and the story one carries a label, so the badge never says "10 TT" on a story option.
3. **The unread range.** Some pages carry `?` (`Spark 1–2?`, `Scrap 26–43?`, `Unlived 28–394?`, `Memory of Light 3–4?`); those are `?`.
4. **Forms strain an aspect** and lock the next form; the same-aspect option is not offered. A form badge should say the aspect.
5. **Generic titles** (`Forget`, `Escape`, `Follow her`, `Negotiate`, `Defy`) collide with other features' names in the file
   (`Follow her` at `:9970`, `Escape` at `:874`, `Negotiate` seven times). Keying on the **card** (an opened-card lookup) is exact;
   a table entry must never match by name alone.
6. **Persistent state:** the Marrow currencies are temporary and reset at the mooring; the guide says none is directly sellable.

## 6. Gating

Card and storylet names as the heading (the cards are drawn only inside a corpse, so the card name is the gate). **Only the game can tell:**
that the opened card's heading is the page title (and *The Hunger* / *The Oath* without the parenthetical), and the sidebar greeting
inside the Marrow ("The Corpse …" is the guide's `GuideFor` list; they are ordinary phrases, so at most confirm-only).

## 7. Badge-meaning recommendation and decisions

Recommended badge: **Tempestuous Tales per play**, a range from the option page's own quantities through the page's own formula,
`TT 10`, `TT 10–14`, `TT ?5–9`, with the payload in the tooltip (`Mote ×1, Spark ×5–9`) and the guide's ≈ 0.5 E per Tale labelled the
guide's figure. Options that pay only a quality carry a `label`-coloured summary (`story step`) and their requirement. Forms carry the
corpse and the aspect they strain. Colour: the shared `CAROUSEL_COLOR_*` palette; the ranges, `?` and `≈` carry the meaning.

1. **Panel: none** (decided 2026-09-29).
2. **Scope — Tale-paying card options, story options as labels, forms as labels (recommended)**, vs Tale-paying options only.
3. **The number — Tempestuous Tales (recommended)**, exact and on the page, with the Echo rate in the tooltip, vs the guide's Echo values only (the guide has seven conversions and marks some "assumed").
4. **Best card — badge *Decipher the message* on the page's numbers (recommended) and say the guide disagrees in the tooltip**, vs leave it unbadged until a capture confirms the range.
5. **Forms — label with the corpse and the strained aspect (recommended)**, vs a summary value per corpse (the mean of its three cards), vs leave the forms out.

**Only the game can tell:** the real range of *Decipher the message*, the headings of the ten corpses' cards, and whether a locked option is drawn greyed.

## 8. Decisions (2026-09-29)

1. Panel: none.
2. Scope: **Tale-paying card options, story options as labels with their requirement, forms as labels**. `Consume what is not there` stays a row of the Firmament feature.
3. Number: **Tempestuous Tales per play** from the page's own formula; `?` where the page marks a range unsure; the ≈ 0.5 E per Tale labelled the guide's figure, in the tooltip.
4. *Decipher the message*: **badge the page's 10–14 TT**, the tooltip states that the guide's "best card" claim disagrees.
5. Forms: **label with the corpse and the aspect strained**; no derived value.
